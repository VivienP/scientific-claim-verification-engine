Patient-reported quality of life improvements with mindfulness-based stress reduction have been demonstrated in multiple randomised trials of breast cancer survivors. Hoffman and colleagues (2012) reported a 38% reduction in fatigue scores at 12 weeks among 229 women with stage I-III disease [8]. A subsequent meta-analysis by Cramer et al. (2015) pooling 12 trials confirmed a moderate effect on depression symptoms (standardised mean difference -0.41, 95% CI -0.62 to -0.20) across diverse cancer populations [11].
